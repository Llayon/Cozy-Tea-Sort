# Sinking honey (Gauntlet 7 — «Мёд на дне»)

## Mechanic
Honey belongs to exactly one vessel and sinks to the bottom. Pouring tea
OUT leaves honey behind while any tea remains; the final outflow that
completely empties the host carries honey to the destination, where it
sinks again. Inflow never displaces honey. Honey uses no tea capacity and
is not a TeaId. Final goal: honey under full homogeneous buckwheat in a
plain standard normal cup.

## Contrast with lemon
Lemon floats and rides ANY outflow immediately. Honey stays while tea
remains and moves ONLY on empty — the opposite trigger, taught by mixed
full initial hosts (first outflow necessarily stays, later emptying moves).

## Production
Tight-loose 2-empty families (4c/6v, 5c/7v+mystery, teapot 4c/6v with
honey starting inside the teapot). Every template demonstrates stay + move
and finishes the buckwheat goal. Gate pools: A 316 / B 298 / C 429
canonical-distinct participating candidates per 1000 seeds.

## Bands (pours)
- challenge 4c/6v: sweet 10–13, accept 8–15.
- peak 5c/7v: sweet 14–17, accept 12–19.
- teapot 4c/6v: sweet 10–13, accept 8–15.
